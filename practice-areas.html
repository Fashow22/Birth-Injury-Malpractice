Catastrophic Birth Injuries We Handle

Eight focused practice areas, each backed by Herb Borroto's M.D./J.D. record review and Alex Alvarez's Board Certified trial work.

Cerebral Palsy

A lifelong movement disorder caused by brain damage before, during, or shortly after birth. Many cases trace to preventable oxygen deprivation during labor.

Learn more

HIE & Birth Asphyxia

Hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy — brain injury from oxygen deprivation during labor or delivery. Often preventable with timely C-section or proper monitoring.

Learn more

Erb's Palsy & Brachial Plexus

Nerve damage to the baby's shoulder, arm, and hand caused by excessive force during shoulder dystocia. Often results in lifelong weakness or paralysis.

Learn more

Fetal Monitoring Failures

Failure to recognize, interpret, or escalate non-reassuring fetal heart rate tracings. The single most common cause of preventable birth injury litigation.

Learn more

Vacuum & Forceps Injuries

Skull fractures, intracranial hemorrhage, and brain injury caused by improper use of assisted delivery devices. Often used when a C-section should have been performed.

Learn more

Defective Birth Products

Defective fetal monitors, vacuum extractors, epidural catheters, and prescription drugs taken during pregnancy. Product liability claims layered on top of malpractice.

Learn more

Hospital & Nursing Negligence

Understaffed L&D units, missed shift handoffs, failure to escalate, and chain-of-command breakdowns. The hospital itself is often a primary defendant.

Learn more

Infant Wrongful Death

When negligence costs a baby's life, the family has the right to seek accountability and recover damages for funeral costs, lost companionship, and emotional suffering.

Learn more

Don't See Your Situation Listed?

Catastrophic birth injury cases come in many forms. If something went wrong during your pregnancy, labor, delivery, or your baby's early days — and you suspect it wasn't supposed to happen — we want to hear from you. The consultation is free.
