What to Have Ready

It speeds things up if you have these, but don't worry if you don't — we'll get them.

  • The hospital where your baby was delivered
  • The approximate date of delivery
  • The name of the OB or attending physician (if you remember)
  • Your child's current diagnosis (cerebral palsy, HIE, etc.)
  • Any records you have already obtained

Time matters in birth injury cases.

Statutes of limitations vary by state — some have hard deadlines, others give extra time for injuries to minors. Records can disappear and witness memories degrade. The earlier we start, the stronger the case.
